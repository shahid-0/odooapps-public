🚀 How It Works

  • Step 1: Install the Sticky Notes module.
  • Step 2: Click the note icon in your systray.
  • Step 3: Choose Add Note to create or View Notes to manage.
  • Step 4: Notes are automatically saved and accessible anytime.

🧩 Compatibility

Works seamlessly with Odoo 17, 18, and 19 — both Community and Enterprise editions.
