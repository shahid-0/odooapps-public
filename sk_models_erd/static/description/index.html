Model ERD Diagram

Visualize Odoo Models and Relationships with Interactive ERD

Interactive Visualization

Instantly generate Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD) for any Odoo model. Understand complex relationships at a glance with a clean, interactive diagram powered by GoJS.

  • Visualize One2many, Many2one, and Many2many fields.
  • Drag and rearrange nodes for better visibility.
  • Expand/Collapse field lists to focus on what matters.

Color-Coded & Clear

The diagram uses a smart color-coding system to differentiate field types and making the schema easy to read.

  • Blue Standard Fields
  • Purple Relational Fields

Link labels are clearly visible with background masking, preventing crossing lines from obscuring field names.

One-Click Export

Need to share the schema or include it in documentation?

Download the high-quality PNG image of the entire diagram with a single click using the built-in Download Image button.
